How MediR Works

A simple 4-step AI pipeline making complex medical documents accessible to everyone.

1. Upload Your Report

Upload lab tests, doctor notes, or prescriptions in PDF, PNG, JPG format, or snap a live photo.

2. AI Understands Info

Intelligent OCR and medical AI extract parameters, check reference bounds, and flag high/low values.

3. Get Simple Explanations

Complex medical jargon is broken down into simple terms with custom questions for your doctor.

4. Listen in Your Language

Read or listen in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Punjabi, Kannada, or Malayalam.
